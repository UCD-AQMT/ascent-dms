The ASCENT (Atmospheric Science and Chemistry mEasurement NeTwork) data are freely 
available to the public and the scientific community. Since these data are the product
of extensive and continuous effort by the team to maintain ASCENT instrumentation and
ensure the collection and processing of high-quality data, we expect users of these data
to ensure that the responsible ASCENT team members receive fair credit for their work
and are offered the opportunity to contribute to the data analysis and co-author 
manuscripts, as appropriate. Note that co-authorship eligibility is not limited to 
writing sections of a paper but rather it encompasses the broader contribution to data
collection and analysis.

Early communication with ASCENT team members and individual site PIs (contact information
available below) is expected to ensure the quality and limitations of the data are 
accurately represented in the planned research.

When using these data in a publication or presentation, we require that you:
1. Include this statement in the acknowledgement section: “The ASCENT data used in this 
work were supportedby National Science Foundation (NSF) grants 2131914 and 2624916.”

2. Cite the DOI for each ASCENT dataset used in your work (four citations total, one for 
each instrument). Use the citation text provided under the Citation tab on each dataset's
GDEX page. The DOI is also available above.
